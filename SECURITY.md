## Overview

The app we developed is open-source. Its amazing advantage is that it does not connect to any servers; your information remains entirely between the two parties involved.

Secondly, the data transmission operates by setting up a local server on your computer; since you do not connect to any external servers, the security of your information is guaranteed to the highest possible degree.

Your chat messages are initiated by you and the other party, so no one else can extract information from the conversation—unless it is the other party doing so.

You can upload anything; if you would like to add new features, please visit https://github.com/creaddinscart/ChatClient/issues
